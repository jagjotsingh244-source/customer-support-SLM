# Returning electronics

## Return window
You can return unopened electronics within 7 days of delivery.

## Opened electronics
Opened electronics can be returned only if they are faulty, within 7 days of
delivery. Opened electronics that work as described cannot be returned for a
change of mind.

## How we check a fault
We check the fault during inspection, within 2 days of pickup. If the item is
faulty, we send your refund. If it is not faulty, the return is rejected and we
send the item back to you.

## Earbuds, earphones and trimmers
In-ear earbuds, earphones and beard trimmers are marked "Not returnable" once
unsealed, for hygiene reasons (see "Items you cannot return"). If one arrives
faulty, damaged in transit or as the wrong item, tell us within 7 days of
delivery and we replace it after inspection.

## After the 7-day return window
Electronics more than 7 days after delivery cannot be returned. If the item
develops a fault, it may be covered by its warranty (see "Warranty").

## Electronics in a split order
If electronics arrived in a different shipment from the rest of your order, the
7-day return window starts on the delivery date of that shipment.
