# Shipping times

## When your order ships
We hand your order to the carrier 1 day after you place it.

## Delivery time to metro cities
Delivery to metro cities takes 2–4 days after the order ships. Metro cities are
Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad.

## Delivery time to other cities
Delivery to all other cities takes 4–7 days after the order ships.

## Estimated delivery date
Once your order ships, its tracking page shows an estimated delivery date: the
day the order should arrive. Nova quotes this date and does not give a different
one.

## If the estimated delivery date has passed
The shipment is delayed. We cannot promise a new delivery date; Nova shares
the last tracking update (see "Delays and lost shipments").

## Orders in more than one shipment
Each shipment has its own estimated delivery date (see "Split shipments").
