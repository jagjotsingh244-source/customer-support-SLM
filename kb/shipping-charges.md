# Shipping charges

## Free shipping
Shipping is free when the items in your order total more than ₹499.

## Orders of ₹499 or less
Orders whose items total ₹499 or less have a ₹49 shipping charge. An order of
exactly ₹499 pays the ₹49 shipping charge, so its total is ₹548.

## One charge per order
The shipping charge is added once per order, even if the order arrives in more
than one shipment.

## Shipping charge on cancellations and returns
The shipping charge is refunded when you cancel an order before it ships. It is
not refunded when you return items (see "Partial refunds").

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery orders pay the same shipping charge as other orders. There is
no extra cash on delivery charge.
