# Split shipments

## Why an order arrives in more than one shipment
Items in one order may ship from different warehouses or on different days. Each
shipment travels separately.

## Tracking each shipment
Each shipment has its own tracking number, carrier and estimated delivery date.
The order page lists which items are in which shipment.

## When the later shipment ships
A later shipment usually ships 1–2 days after the first one.

## Order status while shipments are still on the way
The order shows "Shipped" until every shipment is delivered. One delivered
shipment does not mean the whole order has arrived.

## Return window for split orders
Each item's return window starts on the delivery date of the shipment it arrived
in. If your order came in two shipments on different days, the date that counts
for returning each item is the day its own shipment was delivered.

## Shipping charge
The shipping charge is added once per order, not once per shipment.
