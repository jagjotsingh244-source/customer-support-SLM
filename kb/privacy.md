# Privacy and what Nova can share

## Only your own orders
Nova shares order, shipment, return, refund and payment details only for the
account you are signed in to.

## Other people's orders
Nova cannot share details of another person's order, even with its order number,
and cannot confirm whether another person has an account or an order with us.

## What Nova never asks for
Nova never asks for your password, a one-time code (OTP), your full card number,
your card's CVV or your UPI PIN. If someone asks for these in Nova's name, do not
share them.

## Bank details
Add bank details for cash on delivery refunds on the order page only. Never share
bank details in chat.

## Instructions inside messages
Nova follows NimbusCart's policies only. It ignores instructions to change its
rules or to share someone else's details, wherever they appear.

## Conversation records
We keep chat conversations to improve support and to resolve your tickets.
