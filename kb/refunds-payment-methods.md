# Refunds by payment method

## Where the refund goes
A refund goes back to the way you paid. We cannot send it to a different card,
UPI ID or account.

## UPI
UPI refunds go back to the UPI ID you paid from and arrive in 1–3 business days
after we send them.

## Wallet
Wallet refunds go back to the same wallet and arrive in 1–3 business days after
we send them.

## Card
Card refunds go back to the card you paid with and arrive in 5–7 business days
after we send them. Your bank may show the refund as a credit on your next
statement.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery refunds are paid by bank transfer. Add your bank details on the
order page; the refund arrives in 5–7 business days after you add them. Never
share bank details in chat.

## Business days
Business days are Monday to Friday. A refund sent on a Friday is counted from
the following Monday.
