# Warranty

## Warranty length
Each product page shows its warranty length, from 0 to 36 months. Items with a
0-month warranty have no warranty.

## When the warranty starts
The warranty starts on the delivery date of the shipment the item arrived in.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It does not cover physical damage
(such as a cracked screen after the phone or device falls), liquid damage or wear
from normal use.

## Warranty or return
Inside the return window, a faulty item is returned for a refund (see "Returning
an item"). After the return window, a faulty item is covered by its warranty.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact the support team with your order number and a description of the fault
(see "Contacting a human"). The support team answers a warranty claim within 3
business days and arranges a repair or a replacement.

## Not-returnable items
Items marked "Not returnable" keep their warranty.
