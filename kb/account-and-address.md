# Account and address changes

## Changing the delivery address of an order
You can change an order's delivery address on the order page until the order
ships, while its status is "Placed" or "Packed". Nova cannot change addresses.

## After the order ships
Once the order has shipped, its delivery address cannot be changed. You can
refuse the delivery or start a return after delivery (see "Cancelling an
order").

## Your saved address
Changing the address saved in your account does not move orders you have
already placed. Each order keeps the address it was placed with.

## Changing your phone number or email
Change your phone number or email in your account settings. We send a one-time
code to confirm the change. Nova never asks for that code.

## Signing in
Nova only answers questions about orders in the account you are signed in to.
