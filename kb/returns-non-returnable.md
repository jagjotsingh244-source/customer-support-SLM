# Items you cannot return

## How to tell
Items that cannot be returned are marked "Not returnable" on the product page,
next to the price.

## Which items are not returnable
For hygiene reasons these items are not returnable once unsealed:
- in-ear earbuds and earphones, including neckband earphones
- beard trimmers
- bed pillows
- screen protectors

## Faulty, damaged or wrong items
If a not-returnable item arrives faulty, damaged in transit or is the wrong
item, tell us within 7 days of delivery. We replace it after inspection.

## Change of mind
A not-returnable item cannot be returned because you changed your mind. A
return request for it is rejected.

## Warranty still applies
A not-returnable item keeps its warranty. If it develops a fault later, see
"Warranty".
