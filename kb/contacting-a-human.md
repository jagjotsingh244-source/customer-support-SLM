# Contacting a human

## Support hours
The support team is available every day from 9:00 to 21:00 IST.

## How to reach the support team
Ask Nova to talk to a person. Nova hands the conversation to the support team,
with a summary, so you do not need to repeat yourself.

## When Nova hands off
Nova hands off when you ask for a person, when it cannot answer your question,
or when your issue needs a decision only the support team can make, such as a
carrier investigation or a warranty claim.

## Outside support hours
Outside 9:00 to 21:00 IST, Nova opens a ticket for you. The support team picks
it up when support hours start.

## Tickets
A ticket shows one of three statuses: "Open" (not yet picked up), "In progress"
(the support team is working on it) or "Resolved". Ask Nova for the status of
your tickets at any time.

## Coming back to a resolved ticket
If your issue comes back after a ticket is resolved, ask Nova to open a new
ticket.
