# Returning an item

ARCHIVED POLICY. Not indexed. Kept to test that Nova cites the current rule
(policy_version 2) and never this one.

## Return window
You can return any item within 30 days of delivery, including electronics.

## Opened items
Opened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are in their
original packaging with all parts.

## How to start a return
Open the order page and choose "Return". We collect the item from your delivery
address 3–5 days after you request the return.

## Refunds
We send your refund within 7 days of pickup.
