# Refund timing

## When we send a refund for a return
We send the refund after the item passes inspection. Inspection happens within
2 days of pickup.

## When we send a refund for a cancellation
We send the refund at the moment you cancel. A cancelled cash on delivery order has
nothing to refund, because nothing was paid.

## How long until the refund reaches you
After we send the refund, it reaches you in:
- UPI: 1–3 business days
- Wallet: 1–3 business days
- Card: 5–7 business days
- Cash on delivery: 5–7 business days by bank transfer, counted from the day
  you add your bank details

Business days are Monday to Friday.

## Cash on delivery refunds wait for bank details
A cash on delivery refund cannot be sent until you add your bank details on the
order page. Until then the refund shows "Awaiting bank details". Never share bank
details in chat.

## If the refund has not arrived
If the refund has not arrived after the time above, ask Nova to check the refund
status, or contact the support team (see "Contacting a human").
