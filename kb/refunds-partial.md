# Partial refunds

## Returning some items from an order
If you return some items and keep the rest, the refund is the price you paid for
the returned items, times the quantity returned.

## Shipping charge on returns
The ₹49 shipping charge is not refunded when you return items, whether you
return one item or the whole order.

## Shipping charge on cancellations
When you cancel an order before it ships, the refund is the full order total,
including any shipping charge.

## No new shipping charge on the items you keep
We do not add a shipping charge to the items you keep, even if their total is
₹499 or less.

## Example
An order has a lamp at ₹499 and a cable at ₹299, with free shipping. You return
the cable. The refund is ₹299.
