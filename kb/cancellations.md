# Cancelling an order

## Before the order ships
You can cancel for free until the order ships, while its status is "Placed" or
"Packed". Ask Nova or open the order page. The order is cancelled only after you
confirm.

## Refund after cancelling
The refund is the full order total, including any shipping charge. We send it at
the moment you cancel (see "Refund timing" for when it reaches you). A cancelled
cash on delivery order has nothing to refund.

## After the order ships
Once the order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You have two options:
- Refuse the delivery when the courier arrives. The shipment comes back to us;
  we inspect it within 2 days of its arrival and then send the refund.
- Accept the delivery and start a return within the return window (see
  "Returning an item").

## Split orders
If part of your order has already shipped, the order cannot be cancelled. Refuse
the shipments you do not want, or return the items after delivery.
