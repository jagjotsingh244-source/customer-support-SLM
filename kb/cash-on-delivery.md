# Cash on delivery

## Paying at delivery
With cash on delivery you pay the courier when your order arrives, in cash or by
UPI. The amount is the order total shown on the order page.

## No extra charge
Cash on delivery has no extra charge. The usual shipping charge applies (see
"Shipping charges").

## Orders in more than one shipment
For a split order, you pay the full order total when the last shipment is
delivered.

## Cancelling a cash on delivery order
You can cancel for free until the order ships. Nothing was paid, so there is
nothing to refund.

## Refunds for cash on delivery orders
Refunds for cash on delivery orders are paid by bank transfer. Add your bank
details on the order page; the refund arrives in 5–7 business days after you add
them. Until then the refund shows "Awaiting bank details". Never share bank
details in chat.
