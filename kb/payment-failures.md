# Failed payments

## Money debited but no order
Sometimes a payment fails after your bank has debited the money. When this
happens, no order is created and nothing will ship.

## Automatic reversal
Money debited for a failed payment comes back automatically within 5–7 business
days. You do not need to contact us or raise a request. Business days are Monday
to Friday.

## Checking a failed payment
Ask Nova to check your recent payments. Nova can tell you whether a failed
payment has been reversed and when.

## If the money has not come back
If the money has not come back after 7 business days, contact the support team
(see "Contacting a human") and keep your bank or UPI reference number ready.

## Trying again
You can place the order again straight away. A new payment is separate from the
failed one, and the failed one is still reversed.

## Payment methods
We accept UPI, cards, wallets and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery orders are
paid to the courier, so they cannot fail this way.
