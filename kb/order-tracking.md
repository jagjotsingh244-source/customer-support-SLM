# Tracking an order

## Where to find tracking
Ask Nova with your order number, or open the order page. Tracking appears once
the order ships.

## Carriers and tracking numbers
We ship with two carriers. BlueLine tracking numbers start with BL and SwiftShip
tracking numbers start with SS, followed by 8 digits.

## Order statuses
- Placed: we have your order.
- Packed: your items are packed and waiting for the carrier.
- Shipped: the carrier has at least one shipment.
- Out for delivery: the courier is bringing it today.
- Delivered: every shipment in the order has been delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled before it shipped.
- Return requested: a return has been confirmed and is waiting for pickup or
  inspection.
- Refunded: a return was accepted and its refund has been sent.

## Shipment statuses
- Label created: the shipment is ready but the carrier does not have it yet.
- In transit: the carrier has the shipment.
- Out for delivery: the courier is bringing it today.
- Delivered: the shipment has been delivered.
- Lost: the carrier has confirmed the shipment is lost.
- Cancelled: the shipping label was cancelled with the order.

## Tracking updates
Each scan by the carrier adds an update with the time and place. If there has
been no update for 7 days, see "Delays and lost shipments".

## Orders in more than one shipment
Each shipment in an order has its own tracking number. The order shows
"Shipped" until every shipment is delivered (see "Split shipments").
