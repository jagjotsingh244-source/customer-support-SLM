# Delays and lost shipments

## A delayed shipment
A shipment is delayed when its estimated delivery date has passed and it has not
been delivered. Its tracking page still shows the last scan and where it was.

## No new delivery date for delayed shipments
For a delayed shipment we cannot promise a new delivery date. Nova shares the
last tracking update and does not guess a new date.

## A possible loss
A shipment with no tracking update for 7 days counts as a possible loss. The
support team opens a carrier investigation.

## Carrier investigation
The carrier investigation gives a result within 5 business days. If the shipment
is confirmed lost, you choose a full refund for the lost items or a replacement.

## Delivered but not received
If tracking shows "Delivered" but you did not get the shipment, first check with
your household, neighbours and building security. If you still cannot find it,
report it within 48 hours of the "Delivered" scan. The support team opens a
ticket and asks the carrier for proof of delivery.

## Damaged on arrival
If a shipment arrives damaged, start a return with the reason "damaged in
transit" within the item's return window (see "Returning an item").
